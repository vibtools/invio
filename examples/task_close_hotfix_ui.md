# Windows Task Close Canary — v1.0.0.1.48.01

1. Open a Ready, Stopped, Failed or Completed Task.
2. Click **Close Task**.
3. Confirm that the custom dark confirmation dialog is visible and interactive.
4. Choose **No** and confirm the Task remains.
5. Click **Close Task** again and choose **Yes**.
6. Confirm the Task card disappears, reserved accounts become available, Reports refresh, and Live Logs records `Task N closed; reserved accounts released.`
7. Confirm Running/Paused/Stopping Tasks remain blocked from close until stopped.
